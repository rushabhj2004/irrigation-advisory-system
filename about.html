About the Irrigation Advisory System

The AI-Based Irrigation Advisory System is designed to support sugarcane farmers by combining Artificial Intelligence, IoT Sensors, and Weather Forecasting to make irrigation decisions more accurate, efficient, and sustainable.

Project Overview

Traditional irrigation methods often result in overwatering or underwatering due to a lack of real-time field information. This leads to water wastage and lower crop productivity.

Our proposed solution continuously collects soil moisture, humidity, temperature, and weather information through IoT sensors. AI algorithms process these parameters and recommend the best irrigation schedule for sugarcane cultivation.

The system improves water efficiency, reduces manual effort, lowers farming costs, and increases crop yield.
