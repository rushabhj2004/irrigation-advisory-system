AI-Based Irrigation Advisory System for Sugarcane Crop

An intelligent irrigation advisory platform that combines Artificial Intelligence, IoT Sensors, Weather Forecasting and Soil Moisture Monitoring to optimize water usage, improve crop health, and increase sugarcane productivity.

Core Features

AI Prediction

AI analyzes moisture level, weather conditions and crop requirements to recommend the ideal irrigation schedule.

IoT Sensors

Real-time monitoring using soil moisture, humidity and temperature sensors for precise irrigation.

Weather Forecast

Weather prediction helps reduce unnecessary irrigation before rainfall.

Water Conservation

Reduce water wastage by supplying only the required amount of water to crops.

About the Project

The AI-Based Irrigation Advisory System is designed specifically for sugarcane farmers. Using IoT sensors installed in agricultural fields, the system continuously monitors soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and weather conditions. Artificial Intelligence analyzes these parameters and generates irrigation recommendations, ensuring efficient water management, higher crop yield, reduced operational cost, and sustainable farming.
